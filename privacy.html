Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Information we collect

Slate does not collect personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics. Players, matches, stats and settings are stored only on your device. The caller voice uses the speech built into your device.

Purchases

Slate Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple’s App Store. Purchases are validated through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt — never your name or email. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Children

Slate is not directed at children under 13.
